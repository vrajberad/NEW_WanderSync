# WanderSync Vendor Portal

---

# Purpose

The Vendor Portal is the operational dashboard for travel partners.

It enables vendors to create, manage, monitor, and optimize trips while providing real-time business analytics.

Instead of only uploading trips, vendors manage their entire business through WanderSync.

---

# Vision

The Vendor Portal should become the operating system for every travel organizer.

Every trip, booking, payment, customer interaction, inventory update, and revenue report should be accessible from one place.

# Engineering Principles

The Vendor Portal should reduce operational effort while increasing business visibility.

Every vendor should have complete control over their inventory, pricing, customers, and performance through a secure and intuitive dashboard.

The portal should scale from individual tour operators to enterprise travel companies without architectural changes.

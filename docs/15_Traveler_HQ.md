# WanderSync Traveler HQ

---

# Purpose

Traveler HQ is the personalized dashboard of every WanderSync user.

Instead of showing a simple booking history, Traveler HQ creates a complete travel identity.

It combines travel statistics, achievements, wallet management, memories, upcoming trips, referrals, and personalized recommendations into one unified experience.

Traveler HQ is designed to increase user engagement, retention, and loyalty.

---

# Vision

Travel should not end after booking.

Travel should become a journey that users continuously build over time.

Traveler HQ becomes the user's digital travel passport.

# Engineering Principles

Traveler HQ should feel personal, rewarding, and engaging.

It should motivate users to return even when they are not actively planning a trip.

Every completed journey should strengthen the user's travel identity and deepen their relationship with the WanderSync ecosystem.

# Product Goal

Build India's most intelligent AI-powered experiential travel platform.

The product should combine:

• OTA (MakeMyTrip)
• Tours & Travels
• AI Trip Planner
• Group Collaboration
• Custom Trip Builder
• Vendor Marketplace
• Traveler Dashboard
• Gamification

into one seamless platform.
